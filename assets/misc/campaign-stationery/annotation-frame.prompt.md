# Archive annotation frame

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Transparent PNG master copied into the project; nine-slice rendering preserves corners around live text. Light/dark paper fills reuse the existing ImageGen archive stationery.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: transparent production UI border for nine-slice scaling of quiet archive annotation strips in an Arkham Horror campaign journal website.
Primary request: a square antique bronze-gold engraved rectangular frame, on a genuinely transparent background, with a completely empty transparent center. Render only the border artwork. Fine understated archival manuscript trim, matching aged parchment, tarnished gold bookbinding and muted olive-brown archive paper.
Composition: exact square, orthographic top-down, entire delicate frame fully visible. Straight narrow horizontal and vertical border rails extend unbroken between corners. Frame lies nearly at the outside edges of the square with only a tiny transparent margin. Each small corner contains a modest curled engraved acanthus flourish, confined to the outermost 12% corner area. Thin unobtrusive rails; no ornament at the midpoints of edges. Interior at least 80% of image is transparent. Designed to be nine-slice scaled to long text strips 40-70px tall; corners will be shown at just 12-16px and must remain restrained.
Materials: subdued weathered bronze, dusty antique gold engraving, subtly worn natural metal, matte patina with very limited glints. Neither shiny nor glowing. Same neutral bronze frame must be readable on both light warm vellum and dark umber archive paper.
Constraints: transparent center and outside, no paper fill, no text, no letters, no symbols, no emblems, no seals, no gemstones, no heraldry, no crest, no objects, no mockup, no cast shadow, no colored backdrop, no watermark, no elaborate scrollwork beyond the corners. This frame is subordinate to the live text.
```
