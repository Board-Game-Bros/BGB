# Expedition roster bookcloth

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. The PNG master remains in Codex generated_images; the project uses a 768px WebP material swatch. Reuses annotation-frame.png for nine-slice antique gold trim. Live portraits, names and status marks remain in the page.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: production background texture for the Expedition Team roster in an Arkham Horror antique campaign journal website.
Primary request: one seamless square swatch of aged desaturated slate blue archival bookcloth, predominantly #526066, subtle woven linen fibers bonded onto worn manuscript paper. It will fill very slim horizontal roster strips under live gold names, circular antique gold portraits and small status icons.
Style and material: tactile old expedition journal binding, circa 1920 archival folio, matte dusty blue-gray and muted steel blue. Extremely restrained low-contrast mottling, tiny paper fibers, sparse gentle abrasion, slight smoky faded pigment, luxurious aged craft. Coordinate with dark olive-brown parchment and tarnished gold manuscript trim.
Composition: texture occupies the complete square edge-to-edge, uniform ambient flat light, seamless tile in both directions, evenly quiet middle and edges. No distinct center, no directional shading. All fine texture stays subordinate to readable live text.
Constraints: keep the blue very low saturation; no teal, cyan, vivid blue or purple; no high-contrast stains, no lattice, no diagonal checks, no geometric patterns, no objects, no landscape, no portraits, no symbols, no text, no lettering, no border, no frame, no vignette, no large spots, no gloss, no glow, no watermark. A usable raster material swatch, not a page mockup.
```
