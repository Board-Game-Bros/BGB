Prologue: Disappearance at the Twilight Estate

Status: Completed

Background

Sunday, November 22nd, 1925, Arkham, Massachusetts. A month after Halloween, heavy fog and growing unease hang over the city. At Josef Meiger's Silver Twilight charity gala in French Hill, what begins as a polished social gathering quietly shifts into a night of occult dread, setting the tone for the entire Circle Undone campaign.

Gala Intro Sequence

  • Josef opens the evening with a formal toast, praising the Lodge's civic image while waiting for the arrival of Carl Sanford.
  • Jerome Davids, Josef's assistant, manages timing and guest flow; Penny White works the banquet floor; Gavriella Mizrah secures the entrance; Valentino Rivas is treated as a favored guest.
  • As conversation settles into routine politics and philanthropy, an unnatural mist spills into the estate, and the atmosphere turns from ceremony to immediate survival.

Chosen Prologue Investigators

  • Gavriella Mizrah (Private Security): posted near the parlor and main entry, she confronts a humanoid shape coalescing from ash-colored mist. Her shots pass through it, forcing a tactical retreat up the staircase as the entity continues advancing.
  • Penny White (Josef's Housekeeper): stepping out for air on the balcony, she witnesses cloud-shapes turning into screaming faces and clawing forms. A bloodstained specter emerges beyond the railing, driving her back inside to seek help.
  • Valentino Rivas (Wealthy Philanthropist): in the billiards room, the hall suddenly falls silent and empty. A dark hound lunges from beneath the table, mauling him before he throws it off and backs away from the broken frame.

Scenario I: The Witching Tour

Status: Completed Resolution: 1 Victory Display: 7

Intro Highlights

  • On a cold November night in Arkham, the investigators seek out Anna Kaslow in Independence Square for a fortune reading and quickly realize this is more than street mysticism.
  • Kaslow lays out a full spread, then reveals the center card: The Tower (XVI), an omen of catastrophe and upheaval that reframes the entire reading as an immediate warning.
  • She insists the danger is real and close, saying the surrounding reversed cards represent hidden forces closing in around the investigators.
  • Lead investigator choice: "This is bullshit." Kaslow responds that fate is not fixed, but the threat itself is undeniable.
  • As the vision deepens, the scene collapses into thunder, rain, and darkness; the team wakes in the mud beside a wooded trail, with no one else in sight.
  • Campaign record from this choice: you have rejected your fate.
  • Chaos bag update: add 2 skull tokens for the remainder of the campaign.

The investigators achieved Resolution 1.

  • At the ritual clearing, armed witches surround the investigators, but the confrontation de-escalates when Anette, leader of the coven, takes control of the exchange.
  • After hearing the investigators' account, the coven confirms the ritual circle was breached and accepts that the unnatural pressure over the woods has been disrupted.
  • Anette warns the investigators to leave and not return, then the clearing is swallowed by twisting shadows and the coven vanishes into the dark.
  • Record: the witches' spell was broken.
  • Under Mementos Discovered, record: Mesmerizing Flute and Ritual Components.
  • Each investigator gains bonus experience plus Victory Display experience (7), for a total scenario gain of +8 XP each.
